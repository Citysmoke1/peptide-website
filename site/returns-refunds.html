Returns & Refunds

Our policy on cancellations, damaged orders and refunds.

Last updated: 7 August 2026

Nature of Our Products

Due to the sensitive, batch-tested nature of laboratory reagents, we are unable to accept returns of any product that has left our facility, whether opened, reconstituted or still sealed, except where required by Australian Consumer Law or as set out below. This policy protects the integrity of our cold-chain and testing guarantees for all customers.

Order Cancellations

Orders may be cancelled free of charge any time before dispatch. Once an order has been packed or dispatched, it cannot be cancelled — please contact us as soon as possible if you need to make a change.

Damaged, Incorrect or Missing Items

If your order arrives damaged, incorrect, or with items missing, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and photos of the affected item(s) and packaging. We will replace the affected item(s) or issue a refund for that portion of the order at our discretion, following review.

Lost in Transit

If tracking shows no movement for an extended period, or an order is confirmed lost by the carrier, we will send a replacement or issue a refund once the carrier's investigation is complete.

Refund Method & Timing

Approved refunds are returned via the original payment method within 5–10 business days of approval. You'll receive an email confirmation once processed.

Australian Consumer Law

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies any consumer guarantee, right or remedy under the Australian Consumer Law that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Because our products are not for human or animal use, no refund or exchange request based on personal use outcomes, self-administration effects, or subjective research results will be accepted.

How to Start a Return or Refund Request

Email us at or use our Contact Us form with your order number, a description of the issue, and photos if applicable.
