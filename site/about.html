Our Story

Research Deserves Reliable Reagents

The Aurum Peptide Co was founded on a simple frustration: researchers were being sold reference compounds with vague sourcing, inconsistent purity, and little to no batch documentation. We set out to build a supplier we'd trust with our own bench work.

Every product in our catalogue is manufactured to a 99%+ HPLC purity standard where applicable, independently tested, and issued a batch-specific Certificate of Analysis before it's released for sale. We publish that data openly in our CoA Library — no certificate, no sale.

We're a small, Australian-based team obsessed with two things: analytical rigour and fast, discreet logistics. If a compound doesn't meet our internal specification, it doesn't go out under our name.
