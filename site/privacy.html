Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: 7 August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order, contact us, or submit a form on this website, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and details of your enquiry or order. We do not collect payment card details directly; payment information (once a payment provider is connected) is processed by our third-party payment processor.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and dispatch your order and communicate about it (including tracking updates);
  • To respond to enquiries submitted via our Contact, Request Product or Track Order forms;
  • To comply with legal and accounting obligations;
  • To improve our website and product range.

3. Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information. Order and enquiry data is intended to be stored in a secured backend database (Supabase); until this integration is live, form submissions on this site are handled locally in your browser and are not transmitted to a server.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

This website uses browser local storage to remember your shopping cart contents and age-verification confirmation. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share necessary details with couriers (for delivery) and payment processors (for order payment) solely to fulfil your order.
