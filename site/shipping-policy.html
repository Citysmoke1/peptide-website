Shipping Policy

How we pack, dispatch and deliver your order.

Last updated: 7 August 2026

Dispatch Timeframes

Orders placed and confirmed before 2:00PM AEST on a business day (Monday–Friday, excluding public holidays) are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after this cut-off, or on a weekend/public holiday, are dispatched the next business day.

Domestic Shipping (Australia)

  • Standard Tracked: 2–5 business days, most metro & regional areas.
  • Express: 1–2 business days, available at checkout for eligible postcodes.
  • All domestic parcels are sent with tracking as standard.

International Shipping

We currently ship to select international destinations. Transit times typically range from 5–14 business days depending on destination and customs processing. Import regulations for research peptides vary significantly by country — it is the purchaser's sole responsibility to confirm that importation is lawful in their destination country before ordering. We are not responsible for parcels seized, delayed or destroyed by customs authorities.

Packaging & Cold-Chain Handling

All orders are shipped in discreet, unbranded outer packaging with no indication of contents. Temperature-sensitive products are packed with appropriate insulation to minimise thermal exposure in transit; we recommend refrigerating your order promptly on arrival.

Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on order weight, destination and selected service. Current promotions or free-shipping thresholds (if any) will be displayed at checkout.

Delays & Lost Parcels

While the vast majority of orders arrive within the estimated window, delays can occur due to courier network conditions, weather or customs processing. If your order is significantly delayed, please contact us with your order number and we'll investigate with the carrier.

Products are shipped for laboratory research use only. Recipients must be 18+ and legally entitled to receive these goods in their jurisdiction.
